# Client and legal approval checklist

## Identity and contact

- [ ] Exact contracting entity and public DBA/name
- [ ] Transaction operator and approved biography/photo
- [ ] Public telephone, email, and business/mailing address
- [ ] Relationship between Sell My Kern Home and GNZ Marketing entities

## Transaction model and claims

- [ ] Whether the entity buys as principal, assigns purchase contracts, double-closes, introduces buyers, or performs another role
- [ ] Licensing and brokerage position, including when any license is or is not being used
- [ ] Permitted statements about offers, condition, fees, commissions, repairs, response times, and closing timelines
- [ ] Assignment/resale disclosures and seller acknowledgements
- [ ] Required California transaction, wholesaling, agency, consumer, and advertising disclosures

## Privacy, consent, and operations

- [ ] Privacy policy owner, consumer-request method, retention/deletion periods, vendors, and cross-site sharing/sale position
- [ ] Operational inquiry consent and separate marketing call/SMS consent language under TCPA and California law
- [ ] Whether marketing calls/texts will actually be sent and by which system
- [ ] Staff access list, assignment rules, export policy, audit review, breach process, and notification recipients
- [ ] Final terms, accessibility contact, and record-retention policy

## Launch authorization

- [ ] California real-estate attorney written approval
- [ ] Owner approval of every page, article, identity item, and image credit
- [ ] Synthetic staging submission and deletion approved
- [ ] Production migration window and rollback owner approved
- [ ] Netlify production deploy, domain/DNS, SSL, GA4, and Search Console explicitly authorized
