# SMKH Neutral Lead Handling and Future Partner Governance

**Status:** Internal safeguard. External partner sharing and compensation remain disabled and unauthorized.

## Neutral lead-handling standard

SMKH administrators must handle inquiries using property- and request-related operational facts only. They must not accept, reject, prioritize, price, route, suppress, or alter service based on race, color, religion, sex, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, marital status, national origin, ancestry, familial status, disability, age, source of income, military or veteran status, immigration or citizenship status, primary language, or another protected characteristic.

Urgency may be used only to identify a caller-stated deadline or safety issue. Financial hardship or another vulnerable circumstance must not be exploited. The system must not infer protected characteristics from names, language, location, household details, or free text.

Current access is limited to authorized internal SMKH administrators. No external recipient should receive a lead until every item below is approved and implemented.

## Future partner-governance checklist

- [ ] Verify the recipient's exact legal identity, contact information, ownership, and accountable person.
- [ ] Document whether the recipient is a licensed professional, property buyer, service provider, contractor, or other third party.
- [ ] Independently verify any claimed California license and responsible broker relationship where applicable.
- [ ] Obtain legal approval for Workside's and the recipient's permissible activities, scripts, advertising, qualification, follow-up, negotiation boundaries, and first-point-of-contact disclosures.
- [ ] Approve a recipient-specific disclosure and affirmative consent process before any transfer.
- [ ] Define the minimum fields necessary for the stated purpose; exclude free text and sensitive information unless specifically required and approved.
- [ ] Prohibit onward sale, sharing, enrichment, automated marketing, and secondary use unless separately disclosed and approved.
- [ ] Obtain written fair-housing, nondiscrimination, privacy, security, retention, deletion, incident, complaint, and audit commitments.
- [ ] Document TCPA/CAN-SPAM and state-law responsibility for each communication channel and technology.
- [ ] Obtain separate approval for any flat fee, per-lead fee, referral fee, success fee, revenue share, or other compensation. No payment is implied by lead delivery.
- [ ] Implement least-privilege delivery, recipient authentication, immutable audit logging, and a tested revocation path.
- [ ] Define how correction, deletion, do-not-contact, and consent withdrawal are propagated and verified.
- [ ] Define complaint ownership, escalation, breach notification, and termination procedures.
- [ ] Test with synthetic records only and obtain owner/legal production approval.

## Prohibited current actions

Do not add partner accounts, partner routing, exports, webhooks, recipient emails, lead marketplaces, shared dashboards, compensation tracking, or automated outreach. Do not repurpose existing seller records for a future partner workflow without an approved legal basis and appropriate consent.
