# Sell My Kern Home search-intent and keyword map

Research date: October 14, 2026. The phrases below are intent hypotheses supported by current search-result language, not volume or ranking claims. No Google Search Console, GA4 history, paid keyword-volume export, or verified ranking dataset was available in the repository.

## Observed evidence

- Current results for Bakersfield seller queries repeatedly use combinations of “sell my house,” “as-is,” “repairs,” “inherited,” “rental property,” and local modifiers. Examples include [Bakersfield Home Solutions' as-is page](https://www.bakersfieldhomesolutions.com/situations/sell-as-is), [a Bakersfield rental-property guide](https://www.bakersfieldishome.com/blog/tired-of-managing-your-rental-property-smart-selling-options-for-bakersfield-landlords), and [a Bakersfield inherited-property guide](https://trifectagroupca.com/blog/selling-an-inherited-home-in-bakersfield-ca-what-heirs-and-executors-need-to-know).
- Many competing results emphasize “cash,” “fast,” fixed timeframes, or no-cost claims. Those claims are not adopted because GNZ's exact transaction role and evidence for such promises are not approved.
- California legal/process content is supported only with authoritative references, primarily [California Courts probate self-help](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/probate) and the [California Department of Real Estate disclosure guide](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/re6.pdf).

## Cannibalization controls

- `/` owns the broad brand/local entry experience; `/sell-your-house-bakersfield` owns a detailed comparison intent.
- The as-is page explains transaction and condition considerations; the repairs page focuses on repair scope and path comparison.
- The inherited page focuses on authority and estate organization; the article serves longer informational research.
- The rental page is the only page targeting tenant-occupied/rental-owner intent.
- Old equivalent situation URLs permanently redirect to their new canonical targets. Vacant-property and relocating pages remain unique.
- Draft articles and all five seller pages remain explicitly unapproved for indexing until content approval, even if the global indexing variable is changed accidentally.
