# Homepage wireframe

1. **Header:** original house-line wordmark, six primary links, property CTA.
2. **Hero:** compliance-safe headline/subhead on sage; licensed Bakersfield house photograph; overlaid two-stage seller form.
3. **Reassurance:** private inquiry, clear next steps, no obligation to submit.
4. **Situations:** five linked cards for inherited, repairs, rental, vacant, and relocation.
5. **Process:** three equal steps: tell us, review, discuss.
6. **Why us:** local focus, property-specific discussion, clear communication, decision respect.
7. **About:** explicit affiliation and pending transaction/entity disclosures.
8. **FAQ:** three expandable questions plus route to full FAQ.
9. **Final CTA:** one restrained path back to the form.
10. **Footer:** navigation, legal routes, affiliation, and review-build gate.

Mobile order matches desktop reading order. The form becomes in-flow below the image. No sticky mobile CTA is used, avoiding viewport obstruction and duplicate controls.
